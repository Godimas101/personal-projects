# Weekday Supper

📊 Protein: ~56g | Fat: ~9g | Carbs: ~59g | Calories: ~535 | Protein Rating: 8/10
*(Per supper: 1 chicken breast + ~1.5 cups roasted potatoes + 1.5 cups broccoli; assumes a ~200g raw breast pan-fried without oil)*

> **Macro notes:** The chicken breast does almost all the protein work (~46g from a ~200g raw breast) for very little fat. Gold potatoes are the main carb source (~49g in 1.5 cups — about 280g raw, or three smallish golds) and bring potassium. The olive oil, red onion, and Montreal steak spice are split five ways, so they add flavour but barely move the macros (~40 cal per supper combined). Frozen broccoli adds fibre and vitamin C for ~40 cal. Breast size is the biggest variable — every 50g of raw chicken is roughly ±60 cal and ±11g protein, and the rating holds at 8/10 for any breast over ~175g raw.

Five suppers batch-cooked in one go, so weekday nutrition stays predictable. Vary the chicken spices to keep the week interesting.

## Components

### Chicken *(batch of 5 — 1 breast per supper)*

- 5 boneless skinless chicken breasts
- Spices of choice — mix it up for variety

### Roasted Potatoes *(batch of 5 — ~1.5 cups per supper)*

- ~15 gold potatoes, cut into chunks
- 1 red onion, cut into chunks
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp Montreal Steak Spice (Club House)

### Frozen Vegetables

- 1.5 cups frozen broccoli per supper

## Method

**Chicken** (batch for the week):
1. Season the breasts with the spices of choice.
2. Pan-fry in a frying pan until cooked through — internal temp 74°C.
3. Portion one breast per supper.

**Roasted Potatoes** (batch for the week):
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Toss the potato and onion chunks with the olive oil and Montreal Steak Spice.
3. Roast 35–45 minutes, tossing halfway, until golden and fork-tender.
4. Divide into 5 portions (~1.5 cups each).

**Frozen Broccoli:**
Cook from frozen (microwave or steam) at serving time.

## Serve

1 chicken breast, ~1.5 cups roasted potatoes, and 1.5 cups broccoli. Cooked chicken keeps 3–4 days in the fridge — freeze the last couple of portions and move them to the fridge the night before.
