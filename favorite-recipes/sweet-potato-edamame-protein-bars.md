# Sweet Potato Edamame Protein Bars

📊 Protein: 21g | Fat: 11g | Carbs: 18g | Calories: 235 | Protein Rating: 7/10
*(Per bar with the generous ZoRaw coating; yields 14 bars from a 9x13x2-inch Pyrex tray. Refrigerate up to 5 days or freeze.)*

> **Macro notes:** Egg whites carry the bulk of the protein, with edamame and hemp hearts filling out the rest. Sweet potato gives moisture and structure without flour. Cacao, edamame isoflavones, and the optional ginger add an anti-inflammatory edge. Coated generously with melted ZoRaw Dark Chocolate Protein Bars for a richer chocolate finish.

Chocolatey, fudgy, and genuinely a snack rather than a meal. Grab-and-go friendly. This is the calibrated 14-bar batch for a 9x13 Pyrex tray.

## Ingredients

**For the bars:**
- 1 large sweet potato (about 1 lb / 450–500 g raw)
- 1 7/8 cups liquid egg whites
- 1 1/2 cups shelled edamame, thawed and well drained (pat very dry)
- 1/2 cup hemp hearts
- 6–7 pitted dates, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup + 1 tbsp cacao powder
- 3 tbsp natural peanut butter
- 1 1/2 tbsp maple syrup or honey
- 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 3/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp salt
- *Optional anti-inflammatory boost:* 3/4 tsp ground ginger

**For the coating:**
- 3½ ZoRaw Dark Chocolate Bars with Protein (52 g each) — melted for full-coverage drizzle or dip

## Method

1. **Preheat & prep:** Preheat oven to 350°F. Line the 9x13x2-inch Pyrex tray with parchment paper (or lightly grease with coconut oil).

2. **Cook the sweet potato:** Microwave 8–10 minutes (or steam) until very soft. Cool slightly, then mash until smooth.

3. **Drain the edamame:** Thaw and thoroughly pat dry to remove excess water — wet edamame makes a soggy bar.

4. **Mix the batter:** In a large bowl or food processor, combine the mashed sweet potato, egg whites, edamame, hemp hearts, chopped dates, cacao powder, peanut butter, maple syrup/honey, vanilla, cinnamon, salt, and optional ginger. Process or stir until you have a thick, fudgy dough that holds together well.

5. **Bake:** Press the mixture evenly and firmly into the prepared 9x13 tray — the layer will be a bit thinner than an 8x8 pan, which is normal and still produces sturdy bars. Bake 20–25 minutes (check at 18). Bars are ready when the top is set and matte with lightly golden edges.

6. **Cool & chill:** Cool completely in the pan, then refrigerate 1 hour for clean cuts. Slice into 14 even bars.

7. **Add ZoRaw coating:** Melt the 3½ ZoRaw bars gently in the microwave (20-second bursts, stirring often). Generously drizzle or dip each bar for full coverage. Return to the fridge for 10–15 minutes until the chocolate sets.

8. **Store:** Refrigerate in an airtight container up to 5 days, or freeze for longer.

## Tweaks

- **Lower carb:** Reduce dates or sweet potato slightly and add more hemp hearts.
- **Higher protein:** Swap an extra 1/4 cup egg whites in for some of the sweet potato, or add a scoop of unflavoured protein powder with 1–2 tbsp extra liquid.
- **No dates:** Substitute 2–3 tbsp additional maple syrup; the texture will be slightly softer.
- **Lighter coating:** Cut the ZoRaw coating to 2 bars for a thinner finish if you don't want the extra fat/carbs.
