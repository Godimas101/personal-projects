# Sweet Potato Edamame Protein Bars

📊 Protein: 23g | Fat: 10g | Carbs: 20g | Calories: 245 | Protein Rating: 7/10
*(Per bar with thin ZoRaw coating; yields 8 snack-sized bars. Refrigerate up to 5 days or freeze.)*

> **Macro notes:** Edamame, egg whites, and hemp hearts deliver the protein in a low-calorie package. Sweet potato gives moisture and structure without flour. Cacao, ginger (optional), and edamame isoflavones add anti-inflammatory benefits. Coated with melted ZoRaw Dark Chocolate Protein Bars for a thin chocolate finish.

Chocolatey, fudgy, and genuinely a snack rather than a meal. Grab-and-go friendly.

## Ingredients

**For the bars:**
- 1 medium sweet potato (10–12 oz / 280–340 g raw), peeled and cubed
- 1¼ cups liquid egg whites
- 1 cup shelled edamame, thawed and drained (from frozen)
- ⅓ cup hemp hearts
- 4–5 pitted dates, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp cacao powder (or unsweetened cocoa)
- 2 tbsp natural peanut butter
- 1 tbsp maple syrup or honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp salt
- *Optional anti-inflammatory boost:* ½ tsp ground ginger

**For the coating:**
- 1–1½ ZoRaw Dark Chocolate Bars with Protein (52 g each) — melted for thin drizzle or bottom dip

## Method

1. **Prep the base:** Microwave or steam the sweet potato cubes until soft (8–10 minutes). Drain well and mash until smooth. Thaw the edamame (microwave 1–2 min or run under warm water), then pat dry.

2. **Mix the batter:** In a food processor or large bowl, combine mashed sweet potato, edamame, egg whites, hemp hearts, chopped dates, cacao powder, peanut butter, maple syrup, vanilla, cinnamon, salt, and optional ginger. Process or mash/stir until a thick, fudgy dough forms — it should hold its shape. If too wet, add 1–2 tbsp almond flour or extra hemp hearts.

3. **Bake:** Preheat oven to 350°F. Line an 8x8-inch pan (or loaf pan) with parchment. Press the mixture evenly (about ¾-inch thick). Bake 18–22 minutes until set and the top looks matte. Cool completely in the pan, then chill in the fridge for 1 hour (makes slicing clean).

4. **Add ZoRaw coating:** Melt the ZoRaw bar(s) gently in the microwave (20-second bursts, stirring often). Dip the bottoms of the chilled bars or drizzle the tops. Place on parchment and refrigerate 10–15 minutes until the chocolate sets.

5. **Store:** Refrigerate up to 5 days or freeze for longer storage.

## Tweaks

- **Lower carb:** Reduce dates or sweet potato slightly and add more hemp hearts.
- **Higher protein:** Swap an extra ¼ cup egg whites in for some of the sweet potato, or add a scoop of unflavoured protein powder with 1–2 tbsp extra liquid.
